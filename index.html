About me

My career has taken me from forensic science to web development to UX design. While those disciplines are very different, the reason behind my work has always been the same: I enjoy helping people. Over the past few years, I’ve become fascinated by how design affects people and enjoy how even the smallest tweak can change a person’s experience. Whether I’m brainstorming ideas to improve a user’s experience, refining a design, or collaborating with others to solve a problem, if something I create makes someone’s day a little easier—or even a little more enjoyable— I’d call that a win!

In my spare time, you’ll usually find me honing my baking skills (I’m into artisanal breads right now), practicing fencing so I can eventually compete, exploring the Bay Area, playing video games, or just learning something new. Curiosity has led me through every stage of my career, and it’s something I hope to never lose.
